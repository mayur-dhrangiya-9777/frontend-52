Top Tourist Destinations in India

Taj Mahal

State: Uttar Pradesh

Best Time: Oct–Mar

Iconic white marble mausoleum and UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Jaipur

State: Rajasthan

Best Time: Oct–Feb

The Pink City known for palaces and historic forts.

Goa

State: Goa

Best Time: Nov–Feb

Famous for beaches, nightlife, and Portuguese heritage.

Kerala Backwaters

State: Kerala

Best Time: Sep–Mar

Peaceful houseboat cruises through scenic lagoons.

Ladakh

State: Ladakh

Best Time: May–Sep

High-altitude desert with breathtaking landscapes.

Manali

State: Himachal Pradesh

Best Time: Oct–Jun

Popular hill station with snow-capped mountains.

Mysore Palace

State: Karnataka

Best Time: Oct–Feb

Grand royal residence with Indo-Saracenic architecture.

Darjeeling

State: West Bengal

Best Time: Apr–Jun

Famous for tea gardens and Himalayan views.
